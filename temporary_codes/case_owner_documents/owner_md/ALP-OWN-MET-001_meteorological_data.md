**Project:** Alpilla 600 MW Combined Cycle Power Plant  **Issued by:** Alpilla Enerji Üretim A.Ş. (Owner)

*CASE STUDY — fictional data prepared for the project environment.*

## 1. Basis
Ten years (2015–2024) of hourly records from the Owner's on-site meteorological mast (installed 2014, 10 m and 60 m levels),
checked against the nearest state meteorological station (22 km) and ERA5 reanalysis. Extremes use the state station's 1960–2024 record.

## 4. Wind
Prevailing directions: NE (poyraz, 38 % of hours) and SW (lodos, 22 %). Lodos storms bring onshore wind with sea spray.
Mean wind speed at 10 m: 4.6 m/s. Basic wind speed (10 min, 10 m, 50-year): 30 m/s. Maximum recorded gust: 38 m/s (SW, 2019).

## 5. Precipitation, snow, lightning
Annual precipitation 680 mm (max 24 h: 142 mm, 2021). Design intensity 110 mm/h (10 min, 25-year).
Snow: 6 days/year on average, maximum recorded depth 34 cm; ground snow load 0.75 kN/m².
Thunderstorm days: 26/year; ground flash density 2.4 flashes/km²/year.

Atmospheric corrosivity per ISO 9223 is C5 (chloride-driven). The Owner considers salt ingestion into the gas turbine a major risk and
expects the inlet air filtration to be designed for the event maximum above.

## 7. Performance test correction range
The Owner proposes that performance tests may be carried out at ambient temperatures between 5 °C and 30 °C; outside this range the
test shall be postponed unless both Parties agree.
