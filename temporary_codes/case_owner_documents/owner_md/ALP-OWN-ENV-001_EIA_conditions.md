# ALP-OWN-ENV-001 Rev 0 — Positive EIA Decision: Conditions Binding the Project

**Project:** Alpilla 600 MW CCGT  **Issued by:** Owner  **Date:** 2026-09-09  **Status:** Issued for tender / contract
*CASE STUDY — fictional summary of a fictional EIA decision.*

## 1. Air emissions (dry, 15 % O2, 70–100 % load unless stated)
| Pollutant | Natural gas | Light diesel oil (≤ 500 h/year) |
|---|---|---|
| NOx (as NO2) | 30 mg/Nm³ | 50 mg/Nm³ |
| CO | 30 mg/Nm³ | 50 mg/Nm³ |
| NH3 slip | 5 mg/Nm³ | 5 mg/Nm³ |
| SO2 | — | 10 mg/Nm³ |
| Dust | — | 5 mg/Nm³ |

- **Stack height: 65 m** (result of the approved dispersion study; the Owner's draft contract states 60 m).
- CEMS per EN 14181 with on-line reporting.

## 2. Water
- Cooling water temperature rise across the condenser ≤ 7 K; temperature excess ≤ 3 K at 100 m from the diffuser.
- Residual oxidant (as Cl2) at the outfall ≤ 0.1 mg/l.
- Process and sanitary effluents treated to the Water Pollution Control Regulation sea-discharge limits.
- No discharge of brine from the SWRO at more than 5 % above ambient salinity at 50 m from the diffuser.

## 3. Noise
- Operation: 45 dB(A) night / 55 dB(A) day at the nearest residential receptor.
- Construction: 07:00–19:00 on weekdays and 08:00–17:00 on Saturdays for noisy works (piling, blasting, steam blowing);
  steam blowing requires 7 days' notice to the village and silencers.

## 4. Marine and ecology
- No marine works (dredging, trenching, pipe laying) between **1 May and 30 September** (fish spawning).
- Fish return system at the screens; intake velocity ≤ 0.15 m/s.
- Marine monitoring programme during construction and for 3 years of operation (Owner's obligation; the Contractor provides data).

## 5. Other
- Traffic: heavy vehicles avoid the village road between 07:30–09:00 and 16:00–18:00.
- Archaeological chance-find procedure applies to all excavations.
