## 5. Hydrogen blending
The network operator has announced hydrogen blending pilots of up to 5 vol % from 2030 and up to 10 vol % after 2035 (not firm).
The plant shall be capable of 10 vol % H2 (contract ER-03.02); the Owner accepts reduced emissions guarantees above 5 vol % if
justified by the OEM.
