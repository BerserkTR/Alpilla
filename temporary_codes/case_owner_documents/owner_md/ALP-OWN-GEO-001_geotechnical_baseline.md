# ALP-OWN-GEO-001 Rev 0 — Geotechnical Baseline Summary

**Project:** Alpilla 600 MW CCGT  **Issued by:** Owner  **Date:** 2026-09-05  **Status:** Issued for tender / contract (for information; Contractor to verify)
*CASE STUDY — fictional data prepared for the project environment.*

## 1. Investigation
12 boreholes (30–45 m), 20 CPTu, 6 MASW profiles, laboratory testing (2025). Grade +15.0 m after Owner's levelling fill (1.5–3.0 m
engineered fill, compacted to 95 % Standard Proctor).

## 2. Stratigraphy (typical)
| Depth below grade | Layer | Key parameters |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.5 m | engineered fill (sandy gravel) | γ 20 kN/m³ |
| 2.5–9 m | loose to medium dense silty sand (alluvium) | SPT N 6–18; **liquefiable under DD-2** |
| 9–18 m | soft to firm clay with sand lenses | cu 30–70 kPa, compressible |
| 18–25 m | dense sand and gravel | SPT N > 40 |
| > 18–25 m | weathered to moderately weathered sandstone/marl | UCS 5–25 MPa |

- Groundwater at +13.5 m (1.5 m below grade), tidal influence negligible, chloride 3,000–8,000 mg/l (brackish) — aggressive to concrete (XS/XA2).
- Site class per TBDY 2018: ZD (Vs30 = 240 m/s). Site-specific PGA (DD-2): 0.45 g.

## 3. Owner's note
Deep foundations (bored piles to the sandstone) or ground improvement are expected for all heavy and vibrating structures.
The baseline is for information; the Contractor is responsible for interpretation and confirmatory investigation (Sub-Clause 4.5).
