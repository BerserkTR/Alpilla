# ALP-OWN-GEN-001 Rev 0 — Site, Existing Infrastructure and Tie-in Points

**Project:** Alpilla 600 MW CCGT  **Issued by:** Owner  **Date:** 2026-09-14  **Status:** Issued for tender / contract
*CASE STUDY — fictional data prepared for the project environment.*

## 1. Site
- Plot area 12.0 ha: rectangle 400 m (east–west) × 300 m (north–south), plant grid origin (E 0, N 0) at the south-west corner,
  plant north = true north. Grid coordinates in metres; geographic coordinates will be given in the land survey (UTM 35N).
- Shoreline 60 m south of the southern plot boundary; the 60 m strip is public coastal land (no permanent structures except
  intake/outfall pipes and a temporary landing structure under separate permit).
- Levelled by the Owner to +15.0 m (completed 2025); compaction records available.
- A seasonal stream runs in a concrete channel (3 m × 2 m) along the western boundary (E 0–10 m), discharging to the sea.
  A 10 m maintenance/flood buffer from the channel is required by the water authority.
- **Usable area** after the coastal setback, the channel buffer and a 5 m boundary landscaping strip: about 10.6 ha.
- Additional temporary construction area: the Owner has an option on a 5 ha field immediately north of the plot
  (across the access road) until Taking-Over; the lease must be exercised by the Owner by 2027-01-31.

## 3. Access and heavy transport
- Existing asphalt access road (6 m wide) from the state road (1.2 km north) to the north gate at E 200, N 300.
- State road has **two bridges limited to 120 t gross vehicle weight**. Components heavier than about 90 t (GT, generators, GSU
  transformers, HRSG modules above 90 t) cannot use the road.
- Nearest port with heavy-lift quay (400 t crane) and RoRo ramp: 18 km by sea, 26 km by road.
- **No jetty or barge landing exists at the Site.** A temporary barge landing on the coastal strip is permissible under a separate
  coastal permit, which the Owner can apply for once the Contractor provides the design (permit duration about 6 months).
