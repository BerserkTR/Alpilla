# ALP-OWN-MAR-001 Rev 0 — Seawater Quality and Marine Conditions

**Project:** Alpilla 600 MW CCGT  **Issued by:** Owner  **Date:** 2026-09-12  **Status:** Issued for tender / contract
*CASE STUDY — fictional data prepared for the project environment.*

## 1. Basis
Twelve-month sampling campaign (September 2024 – August 2025) at three stations offshore of the Site (M1 at 300 m, M2 at 650 m,
M3 at 1,000 m from the shoreline), monthly profiles at 1 m, 12 m and 30 m depth; continuous temperature logger at M2 (12 m).
Bathymetry and current survey by the Owner's marine consultant (2025).

**Important:** the Sea of Marmara is a two-layer sea. The upper layer (Black Sea origin, brackish) lies above a sharp pycnocline at
**20–25 m** depth; the lower layer (Mediterranean origin) is saline, colder in summer and low in dissolved oxygen, with occasional H2S.

## 2. Bathymetry and hydrodynamics
- Seabed slope about 1:45; −12 m depth at 550 m, −15 m at 650 m (station M2), −30 m at 1,000 m from the shoreline.
- Tidal range negligible (< 0.1 m). Sea level variation from storm surge and barometric effects: −0.4 m to +0.6 m (100-year).
- Surface currents 0.1–0.4 m/s, predominantly westward; at 12 m 0.05–0.25 m/s.
- Waves: Hs 1-year 2.1 m; Hs 50-year 4.4 m (Tp 8.5 s), SW (lodos) direction.

## 5. Biology and fouling
- Mussel (Mytilus galloprovincialis) settlement May–October, heavy.
- Jellyfish blooms (Aurelia aurita, Mnemiopsis leidyi): up to 20 kg/m³ of seawater locally during blooms (June–September).
- **Mucilage ("sea snot") events**: severe episodes in 2007 and 2021 (May–July) covering the surface and water column down to 25 m;
  the Owner requires the intake and screens to remain operable during such events.
- Fish spawning season (restriction on marine works per EIA): 1 May – 30 September.

## 6. Owner requirements arising
- Intake above the pycnocline, recommended depth −12 m, at least 3 m above seabed, velocity cap, intake velocity ≤ 0.15 m/s.
- Outfall at least 250 m from the intake, discharge in the upper layer.
- Materials: all seawater-wetted parts for chloride up to 14,500 mg/l and occasional H2S.
