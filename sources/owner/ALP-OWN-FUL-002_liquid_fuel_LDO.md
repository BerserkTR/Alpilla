# ALP-OWN-FUL-002 Rev 0 — Secondary Fuel: Light Diesel Oil (LDO)

**Project:** Alpilla 600 MW CCGT  **Issued by:** Owner  **Date:** 2026-09-15  **Status:** Issued for tender / contract
*CASE STUDY — fictional data prepared for the project environment.*

## 1. Purpose and operating philosophy
Light diesel oil is the secondary (back-up) fuel, used when natural gas is curtailed by the network operator or unavailable.
- Expected use: up to 500 hours per year (emergency back-up fuel status under the environmental permit), typically in winter.
- Autonomy: **72 hours of base-load operation** on LDO from full storage.
- Automatic transfer from gas to LDO on loss of gas pressure, without unit trip; manual transfer back to gas on load.
- Start-up on LDO shall be possible (gas not available).

## 2. Fuel quality as delivered (supplier's specification, based on TS EN 590 automotive diesel, class B/F)

| Property | Unit | Limit / value | Test method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Density at 15 °C | kg/m³ | 820–845 | EN ISO 12185 |
| Kinematic viscosity at 40 °C | mm²/s | 2.0–4.5 | EN ISO 3104 |
| Flash point | °C | ≥ 55 | EN ISO 2719 |
| Cloud point / CFPP (winter grade) | °C | ≤ −5 / ≤ −15 | EN 23015 / EN 116 |
| Sulphur | mg/kg | ≤ 10 | EN ISO 20846 |
| Water | mg/kg | ≤ 200 | EN ISO 12937 |
| Total contamination (particulates) | mg/kg | ≤ 24 | EN 12662 |
| Ash | % m/m | ≤ 0.01 | EN ISO 6245 |
| Carbon residue (10 % distillation residue) | % m/m | ≤ 0.30 | EN ISO 10370 |
| FAME (biodiesel) content | % v/v | ≤ 7.0 | EN 14078 |
| Net calorific value (LHV) | MJ/kg | 42.6–43.2 (typical 42.9) | ASTM D240 |
| Sodium + potassium | mg/kg | ≤ 1.0 (as delivered, not guaranteed by supplier) | ASTM D3605 / ICP |

| Calcium | mg/kg | ≤ 1.0 | ICP |

The supplier does not guarantee alkali metal content after road transport. Past deliveries to other plants showed Na+K up to
1.8 mg/kg after contamination with water in tanker trucks.

## 3. Supply logistics
- Delivery by road tankers of 30–36 m³ from the refinery terminal (95 km).
- The supplier can guarantee **up to 25 trucks per day** in winter emergencies.
- The Owner will purchase the first fill of the LDO tanks; the Contractor provides LDO for its own testing beyond the first fill.

## 4. Owner requirements
- Truck unloading station with at least 4 bays, metering, filtration and spill containment.
- Storage: two tanks, each 50 % of the 72-hour autonomy, in a bunded area with foam fire protection (NFPA 11 / NFPA 30).
- Water drain, sampling and fuel quality monitoring. Fuel treatment if needed to meet the gas turbine OEM limits at the burner.
- Emissions on LDO: as per the environmental permit (see ALP-OWN-ENV-001).
